What do you need to get started?

A short message with what you want to make, who it is for, and any logos, photos or examples you like. I reply with a plan and price.

Can you handle design and marketing together?

Yes. I can design your packaging, build your Shopify store, shoot product photos, and run email, SEO and ads, all in one workflow.

What files will I receive?

Print-ready PDFs, editable source files, and web or social exports, depending on the project.

How long does a project take?

A logo or social media set usually takes a few days. A full brand, packaging range or website takes a few weeks. I confirm the timeline before we start.
